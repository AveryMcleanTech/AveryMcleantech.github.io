"I want you to stop sports betting"

My screen print is about why you should stop sports betting. Sports betting can hit with the kind of blunt honesty people sometimes need. It can show how the excitement of chasing wins often hides the stress, the money lost, and the way the habit slowly takes over more space in someone’s life than they ever intended. The message works because it’s simple, direct, and impossible to ignore almost like a friend grabbing your shoulders and trying to talk to you. By putting that warning on a shirt or poster, the design becomes a reminder to step back, breathe, and choose something healthier than the constant cycle of bets and losses.
